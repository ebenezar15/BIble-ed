# Phases 4–9: Complete App Implementation Plan

> Owner direction (2026-10-14): "Keep building everything and give me a completed app"; hosting both local and deployed; content beyond ten word studies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-logos-relational-design.md` · Content format: `content/SCHEMA.md` · Authoring rules: `content/AUTHORING.md`

## Architecture additions
- Migration `004_content.sql`: `person`, `work`, `entry` (kind, slug, title, subtitle, summary, refs, lemmas, data jsonb, ord), `content_block` (subject_kind, subject_id, section, title, type, by_person, consensus, body_md, status, author, ord, fts), `citation` (block_id, work_id, ref, url, quote, verified), `user_note`, `cross_reference`, `father_comment`, `name_entry`, `lxx_bridge`.
- Seeded content is imported from `content/*.json` with `author='claude', status='draft'`; rows the owner creates or edits (`author='owner'`) are never overwritten by re-imports.
- New open datasets: OpenBible.info cross-references (CC BY, via scrollmapper), HistoricalChristianFaith Commentaries-Database (public domain; filtered to public-domain translations, excluding machine-translated sources), STEPBible TIPNR proper names (CC BY; descriptions flagged as AI-generated by STEP), LXX bridge from Abbott-Smith "[in LXX for …]" notes in TBESG (CC BY).

## Tasks
1. Content schema + validator + people/works registry (done) and authoring agents (running).

3. Dataset importers: cross-references, Fathers commentary, TIPNR names, LXX bridge + validation checks.
4. Shared UI: `Block` renderer (type chip, consensus, status badge, citations with "Citation to check"/"Source needed", Scripture auto-links), `EntryPage` renderer per kind.
5. Word page: Culture/Translation history/Relational lens/Legal history/Voices/Fathers from blocks; LXX bridge card.
6. Verse page: cross-references, Fathers commentary, literary structure (chiasm ladder), linked blocks.
7. Modules: /books (+ dating, canon, manuscripts essays), /jesus, /parables, /names, /torah (festivals calendar wheel, sacrifices, laws, covenants, tabernacle plan, christophanies, numbers, typology, structures), /history (people & Fathers, timeline, denominations, doctrines), /rome, /explore hub; search across entries and blocks.
